Butter beans with tahini and sun-dried tomato

Link preview image
[source site] [Original recipe title] Open original

Ingredients

  • Extra-virgin olive oil4 tbsp
  • Red onions2
  • Fine sea salt1 tsp
  • Lemon1, for zest
  • Garlic cloves4
  • Sun-dried tomato paste5 tbsp
  • Aleppo pepper2 tsp
  • Butter beans in water2 × 400g tins
  • Vegetable stock cube1
  • Tahini3 tbsp
  • Freshly ground black pepper½ tsp
  • Boiling water600ml

Instructions

  1. 1Warm the oil in a large pan. Add the onions, salt and lemon zest and cook for 8 minutes, stirring, until soft and translucent.
  2. 2Add the garlic and cook for 1 minute, until the raw smell has gone.
  3. 3Stir in the tomato paste and Aleppo pepper and cook for 4 to 5 minutes.
  4. 4Tip in the butter beans with their water and cook for 10 minutes.
  5. 5Add the stock cube, tahini, black pepper and boiling water, then stir until combined.
